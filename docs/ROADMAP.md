# Nigerian Cooperative Management System (NCMS) — Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery & Business Analysis (2–3 Weeks)
Goal: Understand Nigerian cooperative operations and produce the PRD and SRS. Deliverables include stakeholder interviews, workflow mapping, user stories, feature prioritization, data model, risk assessment, and KPI definition.

## Phase 1 — Product Architecture & UX (2 Weeks)
Create the design system, user journeys, wireframes, UI, API architecture, database schema, infrastructure design, and permissions matrix.

## Phase 2 — Foundation (Sprint 1)
Implement authentication, registration, OTP, password reset, 2FA, session management, and role-based access for Super Admin, Cooperative Admin, Chairman, Treasurer, Secretary, Auditor, Loan Officer, and Member.

## Phase 3 — Cooperative Management (Sprint 2)
Support cooperative creation, branches, committees, by-laws, board members, office settings, financial year, and membership categories.

## Phase 4 — Member Management (Sprint 3)
Implement registration, KYC, BVN/NIN, guarantors, beneficiaries, approvals, membership cards, and QR codes.

## Phase 5 — Savings Engine (Sprint 4)
Build monthly, weekly, daily savings, fixed deposits, shares, wallet, contribution rules, and interest engine.

## Phase 6 — Loan Management (Sprint 5)
Support multiple loan products, application workflow, approvals, disbursement, repayment, restructuring, penalties, guarantors, and credit scoring.

## Phase 7 — Payments (Sprint 6)
Integrate Paystack, Flutterwave, bank transfers, wallet, USSD, receipts, and reconciliation.

## Phase 8 — Accounting (Sprint 7)
Implement general ledger, journals, trial balance, balance sheet, P&L, budgeting, assets, income, and expenses.

## Phase 9 — Meeting Management (Sprint 8)
Support AGMs, attendance, agendas, minutes, voting, polls, and resolution tracking.

## Phase 10 — Investments (Sprint 9)
Manage cooperative investments, ROI, dividends, and asset tracking.

## Phase 11 — Communication (Sprint 10)
Integrate SMS, WhatsApp, email, push notifications, announcements, and circulars.

## Phase 12 — Documents (Sprint 11)
Store agreements, receipts, certificates, reports, and generate PDF, Word, and Excel exports.

## Phase 13 — Reports & Analytics (Sprint 12)
Deliver executive dashboards, financial analytics, KPIs, forecasts, and reporting.

## Phase 14 — Mobile App (Sprint 13)
Develop Android/iOS apps for members and administrators.

## Phase 15 — AI Layer (Sprint 14)
Add AI assistant, fraud detection, loan risk prediction, financial insights, meeting summaries, and chatbot.

## Phase 16 — Integrations (Sprint 15)
Integrate NIBSS, Remita, Monnify, payment gateways, calendars, SMS, and email.

## Phase 17 — Security (Sprint 16)
Implement audit logs, encryption, RBAC, MFA, backups, disaster recovery, API security, and monitoring.

## Phase 18 — Testing (Sprint 17)
Perform unit, integration, performance, security, penetration, UAT, and regression testing.

## Phase 19 — Deployment (Sprint 18)
Deploy development, staging, and production environments with CI/CD and monitoring.

## Phase 20 — Go Live
Pilot deployment, migration, training, documentation, support, and Version 1.0 release.

## Phase 21 — Continuous Improvement
Collect feedback, improve UX, expand AI, enhance security, and release new modules continuously.

## Suggested Timeline
- **Q1:** Discovery to Savings.
- **Q2:** Loans, Payments, Accounting, Meetings.
- **Q3:** Investments, Reporting, Mobile, Integrations, Security.
- **Q4:** AI, Pilot, Production, Training.

## Architecture Principles
Multi-tenant SaaS, configuration over customization, API-first architecture, event-driven workflows, and complete audit trails.
